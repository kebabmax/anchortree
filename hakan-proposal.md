# Landed — let's build this together

Hakan, you already said yes on the phone. This is just everything written down so we're on the same page.

---

## What it is

A video interview show called **Landed**, by our company Anchor Tree.

We sit down with people in The Hague — students, working people, anyone with a story worth hearing — and we ask them one question: how did you end up here? Career, life, path, all of it. Real conversations, no script, different café every time.

Long-form interview on YouTube. Cut into clips for TikTok. The Hague as the backdrop. We discover the city, its people, and learn something from every episode.

Think Career Ladder but rooted in one of the most international cities in Europe, where nobody is pointing a camera yet.

---

## Why this works

The Hague has diplomats, ICC lawyers, students from everywhere, people who've lived five lives before 40 — and zero content about any of them. Nobody is telling these stories. We'd be building the social media identity of a city from scratch.

We've got the gear. Two DJI mics, a good phone, and cafés willing to host us. The production is simple by design. The story is the product.

---

## What I need from you

You're not the cameraman. You're co-founder.

Practically that means: you handle the technical side of the shoot, you own the edit, and we build this together. I bring the guests, do the interviews, handle the outreach. You make it look and sound like something real.

The workload: two shooting days a month, three episodes per day, six episodes total. We release five, keep one in the bank. Edit time is roughly 3 to 4 hours per episode — call it 20 to 25 hours of editing a month. Manageable, not crushing, and it gets faster once you find your rhythm.
