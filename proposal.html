The Idea

A video interview show called Landed, produced under our company Anchor Tree. We sit down with people in The Hague — students, working people, anyone with a story worth hearing — and ask them one question: how did you end up here?

Career, life, path, all of it. Real conversations, no script, a different café every time. Long-form on YouTube, cut into clips for TikTok. We discover the city, its people, and learn something from every episode.

Think Career Ladder — but rooted in one of the most international cities in Europe, where nobody is pointing a camera yet.

Why This Works

The Hague has diplomats, ICC lawyers, students from everywhere, people who've lived five lives before 40 — and zero content about any of them. Nobody is telling these stories. We'd be building the social media identity of a city from scratch.

We've got the gear. Two DJI mics, a good phone, and cafés willing to host us. The production is simple by design. The story is the product.

What It Looks Like

Two shooting days a month. Three episodes per day, six total. We release five, keep one in the bank as a buffer. You handle the technical side of the shoot and own the edit. I bring the guests, do the interviews, handle all the outreach and cold calls.

Edit time is around 3 to 4 hours per episode — roughly 20 to 25 hours a month. It gets faster once you find your rhythm and build a template.
